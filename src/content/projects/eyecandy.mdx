I wanted to work on hardcore robotics - build real robots and learn more about the entire field - and Eyecandy was the perfect opportunity to do so. And the cherry on top - the robots are really cute and our mission is to make them cuter and more lifelike. 

Since then, I have worked on a bunch of the robots - planning, designing and building those robots both in hardware and software. And also testing them thoroughly and debugging issues.
